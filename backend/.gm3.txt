fix: make the public smoke test advisory instead of a release gate

The runner cannot reliably reach this host:

    curl: (35) Recv failure: Connection reset by peer

while the same URL returned 200 to two other clients during the same period, so the
site is up and the connection is refused by the host's edge rather than by the app.
A datacenter IP being dropped during a TLS handshake is the edge's prerogative, and
the previous three runs failed for three different reasons that all lived in this
check - a redirect it would not follow, a certificate that cannot match an address,
and now a refused connection. Gating a deployment on a third party's tolerance for
our IP range is not a check, it is a coin flip.

- The deploy step keeps the authoritative check, on the host, and still fails the job
  if the API does not answer there.
- The runner-side smoke test becomes advisory: it retries (--retry 5 --retry-all-errors,
  since a refusal is often a dropped connection rather than an unreachable site),
  and `continue-on-error` keeps it from failing the run.
- A step afterwards reports it either way: a step-summary note and a ::warning when it
  did not pass, so "the deploy is green" cannot quietly hide "the site was not reached
  from here".

docs/design.md now states which of the two checks may fail a deploy and why.
